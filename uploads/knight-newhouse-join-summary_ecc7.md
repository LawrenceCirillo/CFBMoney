## Five notable ability-to-pay findings

1. **Texas** posts the largest absolute headroom: ~$352.5M total athletic revenue vs a $50.0M Athletic roster midpoint (gap ~$302.5M; roster ≈14% of rev).
2. **Ohio State** is next on absolute gap (~$336.1M rev vs $51.5M roster; roster ≈15% of rev) while already spending ~$92.4M on football ops.
3. **Texas Tech** and **Oregon** look most stretched on roster vs revenue: roster midpoints are ~**27.5%** of total athletic revenue (Tech $40M / $145M; Oregon $51M / $185M).
4. **Iowa / Illinois / Arizona** show the most roster headroom as a share of revenue (~10.6–10.8% of total athletic rev), i.e. relatively modest Athletic roster asks versus KN revenues.
5. **Line 44 (Institutional NIL Revenue Share)** is mostly **$0** for FY2025 (pre-House); only a handful of schools show non-zero amounts (e.g. Penn State, Oklahoma St., Louisville, Texas). Do not use Line 44 as a proxy for current roster NIL budgets.

## Method note

Institution titles and `preview_id`s were parsed from the four saved conference list HTML files; Notre Dame and USC were added manually (`/fbs/ind/university-notre-dame`, `/fbs/pac-12/university-southern-california`). Drupal `/search/node?keys=…` did not return `/fbs/{conf}/{slug}` hits for these institution titles, so profile paths were resolved from the public sitemap (same URL pattern). Profile HTML is cached under `raw/knight-newhouse/profiles/` for re-runs.
