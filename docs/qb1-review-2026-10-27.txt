DECISION STANDARD
QB1 is a reviewed current model input, not a prediction of the next game's starter or a claim of a permanent job. A coach's announced choice, corroborated continuing starts, or an established current starter during a documented extended absence can support an update. A passing-log omission or a single injury-relief appearance cannot establish a change. When the latest coach says the choice is open, retain the prior working input and flag it; do not label it confirmed.
All source review is bounded to information available on or before October 27, 2026. Search excerpts located evidence; cited decisions use retrieved article/profile text. No social-media fan posts, Wikipedia, game ratings, or search summaries alone decide a designation or class. No paywall bypass or full-text copying is part of this package.
